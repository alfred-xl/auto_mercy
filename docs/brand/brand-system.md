# Auto Mercy Brand System

## 1. Brand overview

Auto Mercy is the customer-facing brand of **Auto Mercy of God Nigeria Limited**, a Lagos-based dealership established in 2023 and focused on quality foreign-used cars. The identity combines premium automotive presentation with direct, welcoming communication and dependable customer support.

This document is the approved visual and verbal foundation for future website and marketing work. It defines the system; it does not represent a completed website, sales flow, reservation flow, or online-payment feature.

Primary marketing tagline:

> Quality Cars. Trusted Service. Nationwide Delivery.

## 2. Business scope

Auto Mercy currently sells **foreign-used cars only**. The experience must support customers in Lagos and customers elsewhere in Nigeria who need nationwide delivery.

Do not create brand, navigation, category, or service concepts for buses, trucks, trailers, motorcycles, spare parts, rentals, trade-ins, vehicle swaps, or instalment plans. Do not add placeholders for possible expansion.

Commercial and contact facts are governed by [business-profile.md](./business-profile.md).

## 3. Brand objectives

The brand system must:

1. Build trust with prospective buyers.
2. Present foreign-used cars professionally.
3. Increase WhatsApp enquiries.
4. Increase direct phone enquiries.
5. Communicate a real physical presence in Lagos.
6. Support nationwide car sales and delivery.
7. Present a premium but accessible identity.
8. Stand apart from generic classified-listing websites.
9. Keep future website experiences visually and verbally consistent.
10. Support future social-media and marketing materials.

The future customer journey is a planning guide, not a feature implemented in this phase:

> Discover a car → review its details → contact Auto Mercy → inspect or reserve it → complete the purchase → arrange collection or delivery

## 4. Brand positioning

Approved positioning statement:

> Auto Mercy is a trusted Lagos car dealership offering quality foreign-used cars, straightforward customer support and nationwide delivery.

The position is premium but accessible. It must be credible for affordable, mid-range, executive, and premium cars without presenting Auto Mercy as an exclusively high-end luxury dealership.

## 5. Target customers

Design and communication must welcome:

- First-time car buyers
- Working professionals
- Families
- Business owners
- Buyers upgrading their cars
- Customers seeking reliable foreign-used cars
- Customers outside Lagos who require nationwide delivery
- Customers arriving through Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google, or a shared vehicle link

Assume that many visitors are mobile-first, comparison-minded, and looking for quick evidence of availability, price, location, condition, and a trustworthy way to contact the team.

## 9. Logo guidance

### Source asset status

The supplied-logo description identifies a circular red, black, and white mark containing a stylized car, car key, full legal name, and company motto. **The actual logo file and editable source files are not present in the repository or supplied attachment**, so the mark could not be inspected or measured in this phase.

Do not create, trace, vectorize, redraw, recolour, or AI-generate a substitute.

### Required handling

- Preserve the original proportions; never stretch, squash, crop, or rotate the mark.
- Do not replace its typography, remove elements, add shadows, add gradients, apply filters, or place it inside another badge.
- Use the original full-colour artwork on a clean white or sufficiently quiet light surface.
- On a dark surface, use an approved white or transparent-background version once produced. Until then, do not recolour the source or assume it works on dark backgrounds.
- Over photography, use the mark only where every element remains legible and contrast is controlled. Prefer a clear, quiet area; otherwise avoid the placement rather than adding effects.
- Never use the detailed circular mark where the legal name or motto becomes unreadable.

### Minimum size and clear space

Final measurements require the real master artwork and a legibility test. Until that work is approved:

- Treat **120 CSS pixels in diameter** as a conservative provisional minimum for the complete circular logo in digital layouts; do not approve smaller use merely because the file can be scaled down.
- Keep provisional clear space on every side equal to at least **one eighth of the rendered logo diameter**.
- Do not use the complete circular mark as a small navigation logo, 16–48 pixel favicon, or compact mobile symbol.
- Revalidate and replace these provisional measurements in the logo-production phase after inspecting the source asset.

### Future logo variations

The following must be professionally produced from approved editable source files in a later phase:

1. Complete circular logo
2. Horizontal website-header logo
3. Compact logo symbol
4. Transparent-background logo
5. White logo for dark backgrounds
6. Black monochrome logo
7. Favicon
8. Social-media profile version

A proper horizontal lock-up is strongly recommended because the detailed circular logo is unsuitable for small navigation areas. No variation may be created without the source files and explicit approval.

## 11. Colour-usage rules

### Mercy Red

Use Mercy Red for primary buttons, prices, active navigation, selected filters, important labels, links, small details, controlled hover states, and visible focus accents.

Do not use it for long paragraphs, large purposeless backgrounds, every control, small text directly on black, or decoration without meaning.

### WhatsApp Green

Use WhatsApp Green only for WhatsApp buttons, icons, and WhatsApp-specific contact actions. It is not a general accent, vehicle-availability colour, or generic form-success colour.

### Tested contrast pairs

Ratios below use the approved sRGB hex values and WCAG relative-luminance calculations.

| Foreground / background | Ratio | Rule |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mercy Red / Pure White | 7.75:1 | Passes AA for normal text |
| Deep Mercy Red / Pure White | 10.87:1 | Passes AA for normal text |
| Slate Grey / Pure White | 5.31:1 | Passes AA for normal text |
| Slate Grey / Pearl Grey | 4.90:1 | Passes AA for normal text |
| Pure White / Graphite | 17.05:1 | Passes AA |
| Pure White / Forest Green | 5.36:1 | Passes AA for an Available badge |
| Carbon Black / WhatsApp Green | 9.58:1 | Required WhatsApp button pairing |
| Pure White / WhatsApp Green | 1.97:1 | Fails; do not use white WhatsApp-button text |
| Mercy Red / Carbon Black | 2.43:1 | Fails for text; do not use this pairing |
| Metallic Silver / Pure White | 1.39:1 | Border only; never body text |

Re-test any opacity, overlay, hover, disabled, photographic, or newly introduced colour combination. Avoid neon, fake chrome, metallic textures, carbon-fibre textures, excessive gradients, and unrelated accent colours.

## 12. Typography system

### Display family

```css
"Fraunces", serif
```

Use Fraunces for page and section headings, prices, pull quotes, large brand messages, and the Auto Mercy wordmark. Approved weights are 500, 600, and 700; 600 is the default headline weight and 700 is reserved for hero-scale emphasis.

Never use Fraunces below 15px or for body paragraphs, buttons, form fields, navigation, specifications, labels, or admin interface controls.

### Interface and body family

```css
"Inter", sans-serif
```

Use Inter for all UI and body text: navigation, copy, buttons, labels, inputs, captions, badges, tables, metadata, filters, statuses, footer content, mobile interfaces, and admin interfaces. Approved weights are 400, 500, 600, and 700.

### Principles

- Standard body copy is 14–15px.
- Meta text and captions are 12–13px.
- Avoid extremely thin weights and excessive uppercase.
- Uppercase is reserved for short labels with increased letter spacing.
- Keep paragraphs generously led and generally within 60–75 characters per line.
- Typography must remain readable at 200% zoom and on narrow mobile screens.
- Editorial styling must never obscure functional vehicle information.

### Font loading

No local font files were supplied. The existing Laravel Vite font integration is configured to retrieve the approved families from Bunny Fonts during a production build, use `font-display: swap`, request only the approved weights, and emit font assets with the build. A future production decision should confirm licensing and whether to retain this provider or self-host optimized WOFF2 files. Do not download fonts from an unapproved source.

- Dashboard

- Avoid unnecessary filters, unrelated people, and distracting objects.

### Future brand photography

Commission real photographs of both Lagos car stands, cars arranged at the car stands, staff assisting customers, vehicle inspections, vehicle preparation, customer handovers, nationwide deliveries, the owner or team, and customers inspecting cars.

## 16. UI design principles

Future interfaces should combine editorial refinement with practical dealership clarity:

- Large, real vehicle photography
- Generous white space and strong alignment
- Pure White and Pearl Grey primary surfaces
- Graphite or Carbon sections used selectively
- Controlled Mercy Red accents
- Thin Metallic Silver borders
- Restrained corner radii and subtle shadows
- Clear prices, availability labels, and car-stand information
- Consistent image ratios and one outline icon style
- Strong, direct calls to action
- Intentionally composed mobile layouts

Avoid excessive glassmorphism, neon, decorative gradients, heavy black shadows, pill-heavy cards, floating decoration, autoplay carousels, racing motifs, speedometer graphics, carbon-fibre backgrounds, unrelated luxury imagery, overcrowded cards, unnecessary badges, and fake scarcity indicators.

## 17. Button guidance

All controls require clear text, a minimum 44×44px touch target, restrained 8px corners, visible focus, and no decorative gradient.

| Variant | Base treatment | Interaction guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Mercy Red background, Pure White text | Deep Mercy Red hover/active; preserve text contrast |
| Carbon | Carbon Black background, Pure White text | Graphite hover; use for strong secondary actions |
| Light outline | Pure White background, Carbon text, Slate Grey border | Pearl Grey hover; border cannot be the only focus cue |
| WhatsApp | WhatsApp Green background, **Carbon text** | WhatsApp actions only; never use white text on this green |
| Text link | Mercy Red text on a light surface | Underline on hover and focus; use Pure White on dark surfaces instead of red |
| Icon | 44×44px minimum, restrained outline | Requires an accessible name; add visible text when meaning is not universal |
| Disabled | Muted Slate/Metallic treatment | Keep label readable; use a disabled attribute and do not rely on opacity alone |
| Loading | Preserve control width and action context | Use `aria-busy`, a restrained spinner, and clear loading text |

Active feedback may use a subtle `scale(0.98)` or surface change without shifting layout. Keyboard focus uses a three-pixel high-contrast ring with a two-pixel offset; use Mercy Red against light surfaces and Pure White against dark or red surfaces.

## 18. Vehicle-card guidance

A future vehicle card contains only the information needed to understand and open the listing:

1. Actual vehicle image in a consistent 4:3 ratio
2. Text-labelled availability status
3. Year, make, and model in Fraunces as the card heading
4. Price in Fraunces
5. A short set of essential metadata
6. The approved car-stand location
7. Clear **View Details** action
8. WhatsApp action only where it does not overcrowd the card

Keep a clear hierarchy: image, status/name, price, essential details, location, action. Do not make the card resemble a generic e-commerce product tile, overload it with specifications, add fake discount language, or use irrelevant badges. Detailed specifications belong on the future vehicle-detail page.

## 19. Status-system guidance

Every state includes readable text and must remain understandable without colour.

| Status | Treatment | Required label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Available | Forest Green with Pure White text | Available |
| Reserved | Deep Mercy Red with Pure White text | Reserved |
| Sold | Graphite with Pure White text | Sold |
| Newly Added | Carbon outline or Carbon surface with contrasting text | Newly Added |
| Featured | Mercy Red with Pure White text | Featured |

Use a consistent badge shape and optional supporting icon, but never an icon or colour alone. Forest Green represents availability; WhatsApp Green remains exclusive to WhatsApp actions.

## 20. Form guidance

- Use persistent labels for text, telephone, email, select, search, and textarea fields. A placeholder is never the only label.
- Use Inter at 16px for normal input text to protect mobile readability.
- Give fields at least a 44px touch height, 8px corners, a Pure White surface, Carbon text, and a Slate Grey boundary. Metallic Silver alone is too low-contrast to identify an interactive field on white.
- Focus uses a visible Mercy Red border/ring with sufficient separation from the background.
- Help text uses Slate Grey on Pure White or Pearl Grey and remains at least 14px.
- Errors use explicit text, an icon where helpful, and Mercy Red; connect the message programmatically to the field.
- Successful submission feedback uses a clear confirmation sentence and icon on a Pearl Grey or high-contrast surface. Do not repurpose WhatsApp Green as generic success.
- Disabled fields retain readable labels and explain why interaction is unavailable when necessary.
- Checkboxes have a large clickable label area and cannot communicate state through colour alone.
- Consent text must be readable, specific, and separate from marketing copy.

## 21. Icon guidance

Use one consistent outline icon family. Lucide Icons is the preferred future direction, but no icon package is installed or approved in this phase.

Icons support text rather than replace important labels. Keep stroke weight, visual size, alignment, and interaction states consistent. Essential icon-only controls require accessible names and, where useful, tooltips. Do not mix outline, filled, emoji, and unrelated icon sets.

## 22. Layout principles

| Foundation | Approved guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Standard content width | 80rem / 1280px (`max-w-site`) |
| Exceptional wide media | 90rem / 1440px (`max-w-wide`) |
| Reading width | 42rem / 672px (`max-w-reading`) |
| Page gutters | `clamp(1rem, 4vw, 2rem)` |
| Standard section spacing | `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 7.5rem)` |
| Compact section spacing | `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4rem)` |
| Grid gap | `clamp(1rem, 2vw, 1.5rem)` |
| Card / gallery image ratio | 4:3 / 16:10 |
| Body-copy line length | Generally 60–75 characters |

Preserve the installed Tailwind breakpoints unless real content testing shows a reason to change them: `sm` 40rem, `md` 48rem, `lg` 64rem, `xl` 80rem, and `2xl` 96rem. Break layouts where content needs it, not around assumed devices.

Mobile layouts are intentionally composed for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Google, and shared-link traffic. Do not simply compress desktop grids. Prioritize the actual car, availability, price, essential details, location, and contact action.

Sticky mobile actions may contain one or two high-priority actions, respect safe-area insets, remain keyboard-accessible, and reserve enough page padding so they never cover content.

## 23. Spacing principles

- Use Tailwind’s four-pixel base spacing scale for ordinary component rhythm.
- Use `gap` for spacing between sibling items rather than scattered margins.
- Use the named gutter, grid, compact-section, and section tokens for responsive layout rhythm.
- Cards use a 12px radius; buttons and inputs use 8px; small badges use 4px.
- Decorative separators and card borders are one pixel Metallic Silver. Interactive control boundaries use Slate Grey because Metallic Silver does not reach the required non-text contrast against white.
- Use the `shadow-card`, `shadow-card-hover`, and `shadow-overlay` tokens sparingly. Borders and spacing should do most of the structural work.
- Mobile interfaces use at least 16px side gutters and avoid dense edge-to-edge control clusters.
- Every interactive target is at least 44×44px, represented by the `touch` spacing token.

## 24. Motion principles

Motion supports comprehension and feedback; it never delays access to a vehicle.

| Interaction | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Section entrance | 180–260ms opacity with no more than 8px movement; content remains usable without it |
| Button feedback | 120ms colour, shadow, or very small scale change |
| Vehicle-card hover | 180ms border/shadow change and no more than 2px lift |
| Gallery transition | About 180ms opacity; no layout shift |
| Mobile navigation | Up to 260ms opacity and short transform |
| Filter drawer | Up to 260ms with immediate close and focus restoration |
| Status change | 180ms fade with an announced text update |
| Modal | Up to 260ms opacity and small transform with proper focus management |

Use the `fast` 120ms, `standard` 180ms, and `slow` 260ms motion tokens with soft `ease-brand` easing. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, duration tokens resolve to 1ms; future components must also remove non-essential transforms and use Tailwind’s reduced-motion variants where appropriate.

Never use scroll hijacking, large parallax, repeated bouncing, continuous decoration, moving car illustrations, long entrance sequences, layout-shifting animation, or animation that blocks immediate interaction.

## 25. Accessibility requirements

Every future interface must support:

- WCAG AA contrast for text and meaningful interface components
- A visible keyboard-focus indicator
- Semantic and sequential heading structure
- Readable mobile typography and 200% zoom
- Touch targets of at least 44×44px
- Reduced-motion preferences
- Useful alternative text for vehicle and brand images
- Status labels that do not depend on colour
- Persistent form labels and programmatically associated help/error text
- Clear, descriptive links rather than ambiguous “click here” copy
- Sufficient spacing and unclipped reflow at narrow widths
- Logical keyboard order, focus management, and no keyboard traps
- Immediate access to core vehicle content without waiting for animation

Accessibility is part of the premium experience, not a visual compromise.

## Repository audit and intentionally deferred conflicts

The branding audit found these pre-existing starter-state conflicts:

- `resources/views/welcome.blade.php` remains Laravel’s default welcome page, with Laravel artwork and copy, hard-coded non-brand colours, 13px interface text, and long transitions. Its no-build fallback also contains generated Tailwind 4.0.7 CSS while the installed version is 4.3.3. The page was deliberately not redesigned because page construction is outside this phase.
- `README.md`, the Composer project description, and the application-name environment defaults remain generic Laravel starter content. They should be aligned during project-information and application-configuration work without rewriting unrelated setup guidance.
- `public/favicon.ico` is a zero-byte placeholder and cannot be used as a brand asset.
- No source logo, editable logo artwork, source photography, or source font files were present.
- The Vite configuration loads only the approved Inter and Fraunces families through the existing font pipeline.

These deferred items must not be treated as approved Auto Mercy design. They remain unchanged only where this phase explicitly prohibited page building or where a missing source asset prevents responsible production.
